# Project Soul

## Vision
Arcana: The Game of Prophecy. A strategic, mystical trick-taking card game where foresight is power. You predict how many tricks you will win, then try to make your prophecy exact. Precision beats brute force. A premium, atmospheric single-player game against AI opponents, playable on the web, Windows, and mobile.

## Goal
A polished, atmospheric card game that rewards exact prediction and keeps players coming back for another round. It is the flagship game of the 23moments studio and a showcase of its craft. Success is measured in games finished and games replayed, not revenue.

## Persona
Card and strategy players who want a thinking game with mood: people who enjoy trick-taking (Skat, Wizard, Oh Hell) and dark-fantasy atmosphere, playing solo against bots.

## Language
English and German, at full parity.

## Principles
- Precision over power. The core mechanic is accurate prediction; the design rewards exactness.
- Mystical atmosphere. Every interaction, animation, and visual should reinforce the dark fantasy and tarot feel. No generic UI.
- Cross-platform. The experience should hold on touch (mobile) and mouse (web and desktop).
- Clean architecture. MVVM with Provider for state.
- If you cannot prove it works, you have not built it.

## Current Focus
- Close the visual gap between the game board (still casino green with plain cards) and the dark tarot vision.
- A portrait and mobile layout (mobile is a target but the board is fixed landscape).
- Give the AI bots real, distinct personalities.
